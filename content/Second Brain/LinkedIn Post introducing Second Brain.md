My second brain is now LIVE - and here's how it changed the way I work and learn! [Home](https://aneurokumar.github.io/website/)  
  
In late 2022, my overwhelm was coming to a head. Even though I'd been working for a while, I kept losing time and energy reinventing wheels.   
  
"I've seen something about this before, but I can't remember" was playing on repeat when I:  
- re-wrote *the same* SOPs across multiple jobs  
- forgot concepts from books at the most crucial hour  
- misplaced sources, quotes, and "eureka!" insights for articles  
  
My career's now divided into before and after reading "Building a Second Brain" by Tiago Forte. It completely reframed my relationship to knowledge. As I built my personal second brain in Obsidian, I started sharing my insights with others.   
  
Since I had to learn Shell, HTML, and CSS this semester, why not turn it into a website?  I developed the workflow outside of classes, learning git, file permissions, a little javascript, and how to troubleshoot when your latest code broke everything...which happened twice.  
  
The site is like a brain. Some nodes are new, full of potential connections, and others are strong circuits that feed into each other. It will multiply, prune, and consolidate as I grow, and soon host my portfolio!  
  
Check it out here or on my profile: [Home](https://aneurokumar.github.io/website/)
